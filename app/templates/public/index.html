Got Questions?

Everything you need to know before you start.

Running a business on WhatsApp or Instagram shouldn't feel like bookkeeping. Here is how SalesIQ keeps things effortless.

Do my customers need to download an app to view receipts?

Not at all! When you share a receipt via WhatsApp or link, your customer opens a clean, beautifully branded web page showing their items, total, and a verified PAID stamp instantly. No downloads or accounts required.

How long does it take to record a sale?

Less than 10 seconds. Start typing your customer's name or product—SalesIQ autofills previous details instantly. New customer names and products are saved automatically for next time.

Is my business data secure and private?

Yes. Your customer directory, revenue metrics, and sales records are encrypted and stored securely. Only you have access to your business dashboard and financial intelligence numbers.

Can I try SalesIQ before committing?

Absolutely. You can start tracking sales immediately with our free tier—no setup fees, spreadsheets, or credit card required to record your first sale.
