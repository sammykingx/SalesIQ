# SalesIQ - Know your sales, Grow your business
SalesIQ is a digital sales and financial intelligence platform designed to help businesses record, organize, and understand the transactions they make in the course of their business.

The primary outcome of the project is to provide the business with a digital sales book that turns every completed business transaction into a professional, traceable financial record, and then turns those records into meaningful business intelligence.

SalesIQ is designed particularly for businesses that sell through channels such as Instagram, WhatsApp, direct communication, referrals, physical interactions, and other informal sales channels where transactions may currently be completed through direct payment without a structured digital sales record.

The platform will allow a business owner to record a completed sale, generate a professional invoice for the customer, maintain reusable records of customers and products, track subsequent payment and return activity, and access reports and dashboard insights showing how the business is performing over time.

## Core Features and Intended Outcomes

**1. Sales & Invoice Management**

The platform will allow the business owner to record completed sales by selecting or entering the customer, products, quantities, prices, discounts, applicable VAT/tax, and payment status.

SalesIQ will generate a professional invoice containing the relevant business, customer, transaction, payment, and financial information. Each invoice will have a unique identity and accessible digital record, allowing the transaction to be referenced and traced after it has been created.

> *Outcome:
Every completed sale becomes a professional, identifiable, and traceable financial record rather than remaining only in a WhatsApp conversation, bank statement, notebook, or the business owner's memory*.
---

**2. Customer Management**

The platform will maintain customer records that can be reused across transactions. Customer information such as name, phone number, email address, and relevant transaction history will be associated with the customer's sales activity.

Where an existing customer is identified through information such as their phone number or email address, their details can be retrieved and reused when recording a new sale.

> *Outcome:
The business develops an organized digital customer history, reducing repeated data entry while making it easier to understand individual customer transactions and outstanding obligations.*
---

**3. Product Management**

The platform will allow the business owner to create and maintain reusable product records, including product names and relevant sales information.

Products previously recorded in SalesIQ can be searched and selected when creating subsequent sales rather than being manually entered each time.

> *Outcome:
The business can record sales faster and build a structured history of what products are being sold, creating the foundation for product-level sales analysis without introducing inventory management into the platform.*
---

**4. Dashboard & Business Intelligence**

SalesIQ will analyze the sales records created on the platform and present the business owner with an overview of business performance.

The dashboard will provide relevant metrics making it feel like a business briefing with insights including, where applicable:

- Last week sales revenue
- Total number of sales
- Average sale
- Best-selling products
- Busiest sales days
- Comparison with previous periods

> *Outcome:
The business owner does not have to manually calculate or interpret their sales records. SalesIQ transforms recorded transactions into a clear picture of how the business is performing.*


## Overall Project Outcome

At completion, SalesIQ will provide the business with a centralized digital system for turning completed sales into organized financial records and using those records to understand business performance.

The platform is therefore intended to move the business from:

> _“I know I made these sales.”_

to:

> _“I have a record of every sale, I know what has been collected and what is outstanding, I can trace what happened to each transaction, and I can clearly see how my business is performing.”_

SalesIQ's core value can therefore be summarized as:

> *Record every sale. Keep every transaction traceable. Understand your business.*
